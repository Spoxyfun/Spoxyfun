FAQ

Pertanyaan umum.

When is mint open?

Mint is open whenever the status shows Mint LIVE. Follow the official SPOXY X account.

How much does it cost?

Free Mint 0.0007 BNB, Public 0.007 BNB per NFT. BNB Chain gas not included.

What's the difference between Free Mint and Public?

Public has a 60% Legendary chance per NFT. Free Mint can still roll a Legendary at random, with lower odds.

How many Legendary are there?

66 Legendary out of 1,031. The 60% Public chance applies while Legendary remain.

What's the max per wallet?

Max 10 NFTs per wallet, both tiers combined.

Which wallets can I use?

Any EVM wallet that supports BNB Chain, such as MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or Binance Wallet.

Where is the contract address?

The official address is only announced on this site and the official SPOXY X account. Beware of fake mint links.
