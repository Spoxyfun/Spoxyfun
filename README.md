## 📌 Why SPOXY?

Prediction markets today are slow, gated, and full of middlemen. **SPOXY** strips that away: connect a wallet, stake your call, and battle another player head-to-head — winner takes the pool, settled instantly on-chain.

This repo hosts the SPOXY landing page and product documentation.

### ✨ What Sets This Apart

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| 🔓 **Permissionless** | Create a challenge on anything — crypto price action or sports outcomes — anytime |
| ⚔️ **PvP, not pool betting** | Every challenge is head-to-head: your win is another player's loss |
| 🗳️ **Community-verified outcomes** | Disputed markets are resolved by SPOXY Masters NFT holders via transparent voting |
| ⚡ **Instant settlement** | Reward pools pay out automatically to winners — no manual claims |
| 🚫 **No KYC, no sign-up** | Just connect a Solana wallet (e.g. Phantom) and go |

## 🗺️ How It Works

| # | Step | Summary |
|---|------|---------|
| 01 | **Connect Your Wallet** | Connect a Solana wallet and deposit funds. No sign-up forms, no KYC gates. |
| 02 | **Create a Challenge** | Pick a market, define the condition and timeframe, and stake the collateral that funds the reward pool. |
| 03 | **Join a Battle** | Browse the open challenge board, pick a side, and lock in your stake. |
| 04 | **Resolution** | Crypto markets settle via price oracles; sports/off-chain markets are verified by SPOXY Masters NFT holders. |
| 05 | **Payout** | The reward pool distributes automatically to winners — no manual claims, no delays. |
| 06 | **Governance** | NFT holders help validate disputed outcomes and shape platform parameters. |

See [`docs/`](./docs) for the full write-up of each step.
